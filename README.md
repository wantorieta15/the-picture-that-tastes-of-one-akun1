# Golden Hour Honey

[![make.fun](https://make.fun/badge/the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/the-picture-that-tastes-of-one-20261122-2047)

One picture of food, one ingredient: **golden honey**.

A wooden honey dipper pours a slow, glossy ribbon of honey over honeycomb in
warm sunlight. The whole picture is the taste of honey — gloss, drip, gold.
Dark rustic background, nothing else competing with the food.

## The program

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the finished picture to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. The copy is byte-identical on every run.

## The picture

- `assets/media-generation-honey-drip-0-512fec05-8557-443a-b0a8-5d2c3cc68a65.jpg`
  — macro photograph, golden honey drip + honeycomb, 524,487 bytes.
- Output: `entry.png` (JPEG bytes, name stays `entry.png`), at most
  1024×1024 pixels, at most 1 MiB.
